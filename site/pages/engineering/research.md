# Research archive

These studies preserve the evidence and assumptions behind Yoke’s
hardware direction. They are dated engineering research. Product
availability, prices, throughput estimates, and hardware claims have not
been revalidated by publishing this site.

| Study | Questions it addresses |
|---|---|
| [Feasibility](../../../doc/research/FEASIBILITY.md) | Bandwidth budgets, 4K120-over-10GbE paths, and open implementation questions |
| [Hardware survey](../../../doc/research/HARDWARE.md) | Commercial endpoints, bridge silicon, USB paths, and bench-unverified assumptions |
| [Compression](../../../doc/research/COMPRESSION.md) | Codec trade-offs, bandwidth, and proposed operating points |

The current [endpoint roadmap](../../../doc/design/EDGE-DEVICES.md)
connects those studies to proposed hardware. The
[software performance report](../../../doc/design/PERFORMANCE.md)
records reproducible software measurements separately.

For a concise view of implemented and planned work, use
[project status](../docs/project-status.md).
